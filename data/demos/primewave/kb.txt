## About Primewave
Primewave is an **AI solution company** headquartered in **Saudi
Arabia**. We deliver operator-grade systems across two go-to-market
tracks:

### B2B — for projects, offices, hotels, retail, mixed-use
- **Lighting Control Systems** (architectural + facade)
- **AI Systems** (computer-vision rule engines, voice agents,
  building-intelligence dashboards)
- **Vision Systems** (NVR + smart-camera analytics)
- **Low Current** (structured cabling, racks, IP infra, access points,
  intrusion, intercom, IPTV)

### B2C — for residences (villas, apartments)
- **Smart Homes** — full-stack: lighting, climate, audio/video, shades,
  security, door entry, room scenes, voice control.

## Protocols we work with
We design, install and integrate across both wired and wireless
control protocols — chosen per project to match the building, budget
and reliability requirements:

| Wireless           | Wired                |
|--------------------|----------------------|
| Zigbee             | KNX                  |
| Z-Wave             | DALI                 |
| Thread / Matter    | Modbus               |
|                    | BACnet               |

We are protocol-agnostic — projects that mix wired backbones with
wireless leaf devices are common (e.g. KNX bus for lighting with
Zigbee leaf devices for retrofit zones).
